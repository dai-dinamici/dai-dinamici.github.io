The Mathematics Area of SISSA (Trieste, Italy) is inviting applications for a **two-year postdoc position** on *“Symplectic Methods in Hamiltonian Dynamics”* (scientific contact: Gabriele Benedetti).
 
The successful candidate can start anytime in 2027 (preferably before September, and for bureaucratic reasons probably no sooner than March).

The **deadline for sending the application is November 19 at 23:59**. The deadline for the referees to upload recommendation letters is also November 19 at 23:59. Shortlisted candidates will be invited to an interview on December 4.
 
The official advertisement in Italian and English can be accessed at: <https://www.sissa.it/bandi/selezione-pubblica-titoli-progetto-colloquio-conferimento-di-n-1-contratto-di-ricerca-area> Candidates need to submit their application at the following link
<https://pica.cineca.it/sissa/cdr-fe-mate-21-2026>

The gross annual salary is € 29.162,20 over 13 months. If you meet certain conditions (you were resident outside Italy with a research or PhD position for at least two years, you will transfer your tax residence to Italy), you might be eligible for a 90% tax deduction. This corresponds to a net annual salary of approximately € 26.395.

You are very welcome to contact Gabriele Benedetti at <gbenedet@sissa.it> for general information on the position and <contrattiricerca@sissa.it> for specific information about the application procedure.
