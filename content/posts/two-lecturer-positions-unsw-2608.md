The School of Mathematics and Statistics at UNSW Sydney advertises **two Lecturer / Senior Lecturer positions** in dynamical systems.

The School seeks to make appointments in the growing area of operator-theoretic methods for dynamical systems, which covers a broad spectrum from theory to applications, in connection with the establishment of a a new research cluster led by Gary Froyland.

The positions are full-time and continuing, at Lecturer (Level B) or Senior Lecturer (Level C) level.

Salary: AUD 133K–156K (Level B) or AUD 161K–185K (Level C), plus 17% superannuation and annual leave loading.

All information and application form: <https://external-careers.jobs.unsw.edu.au/cw/en/job/542195/lecturer-or-senior-lecturer-mathematics-statistics>

Deadline for applications: **September 10, 2026**.
