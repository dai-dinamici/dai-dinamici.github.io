The workshop **Surfaces in Trieste – Workshop on Two-Dimensional Dynamics** will take place at ICTP in Trieste from **April 26 to May 7, 2027**.

The first week, **April 26–30**, will consist of mini-courses by:

- Juliana Xavier (IMERL-FING-UdelaR, Uruguay)
- Federica Fanoni (CNRS & Sorbonne University, France)
- Ferrán Valdez (UNAM Morelia, Mexico)
- Toby Hall (University of Liverpool, UK)
- André de Carvalho (IME-USP, Brazil)
- Frédéric Le Roux (IMJ-PRG, France)

The second week, **May 3–7**, will be devoted to a research conference with invited talks and poster sessions.

There is no registration fee. A limited number of grants are available to support selected participants, with priority given to participants from developing countries.
